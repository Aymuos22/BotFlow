## COTTON CROP — PEST MANAGEMENT

### Cotton Whitefly (Safed Makkhi / सफेद मक्खी)
**Symptoms:** Yellowing of leaves, sticky honeydew, black sooty mold, premature leaf drop.

**Recommended RCS Products:**
- **SPIDER** (Imidacloprid 70% WG) — 12-36 gm/acre. Systemic action against sucking pests.
- **SECURE** (Bifenthrin 10% EC) — 200-400 ml/acre. Broad spectrum, contact+stomach action.
- **MARSHAL** (Pyriproxyfen 10% EC) — 200-400 ml/acre. IGR — suppresses egg hatching.
- **AK 47** (Pyriproxyfen 10% + Bifenthrin 10% EC) — 240 ml/acre. Best for resistant whitefly.
- **HEERO 525** (Pyriproxyfen 5% + Diafenthiuron 25% SE) — 300-500 ml/acre. Multi-mode action.
- **SINGHAM** (Dinotefuran 20% SG) — 50-80 gm/acre. Fast-acting systemic neonicotinoid.
- **KOHINOOR** (Imidacloprid 17.8% SL) — 20-160 ml. Foliar spray for sucking pests.
- **TRIUNE** (Buprofezin 25% SC) — 120-600 ml/acre. IGR for sucking pests in cotton.
- **BOMBA** (Azadiractin 5% EC) — 160-300 ml/acre. Botanical, safe for bees.

**Spray Timing:** Early morning or late evening. Avoid spraying during hot midday.
**Tip:** Rotate between chemical classes every 2 sprays to prevent resistance.

## SPRAY BEST PRACTICES (KISAN TIPS)

### Spray Timing
- Best time to spray: **Early morning (6-9 AM) or late evening (4-7 PM)**
- Avoid spraying during rain, strong wind, or extreme heat (above 35°C)
- Spray when leaves are dry (no dew)

### How Much Water to Use
- **Knapsack sprayer (16L tank):** Cover 1/5th of an acre per tank
- **Power sprayer:** 200-400 litre water per acre for most pesticides
- **Drip/Fertigation:** Follow product-specific rates

### Mixing Tips (Dawai Milane ka Tarika)
1. Fill tank with half the water first
2. Add the pesticide/fertilizer and mix well
3. Top up with remaining water
4. **Never mix two pesticides unless confirmed compatible**
5. Add **PLANTA** spreader (1 ml per 3L) for better coverage

### Safety Precautions (Suraksha)
- Always wear gloves, mask, and protective clothing
- Do not spray against the wind direction
- Wash hands and face after spraying
- Do not eat, drink or smoke while spraying
- Store chemicals in original containers away from children
- If accidentally swallowed: call 1800-313-3036 immediately

### Resistance Management
- Rotate insecticide groups every 2-3 sprays
- Cotton Whitefly: Rotate MARSHAL (IGR) → SINGHAM (Neonicotinoid) → AK 47 (IGR+Pyrethroid)
- Bollworm: Rotate SLAYER (Emamectin) → MOGLI (Diamide) → SARDAR PLUS (OP+Pyrethroid)

## COMMON FARMER QUESTIONS (FAQ)

**Q: Cotton mein safed makkhi bahut lag gayi, kya karein?**
A: AK 47 (Pyriproxyfen + Bifenthrin) 240 ml/acre ya HEERO 525 300-500 ml/acre spraye karein. Subah ya shaam ko spray karein, teez dhoop mein nahi.

**Q: Paddy mein tana chedak rog ho raha hai?**
A: MOGLI GR (Chlorantraniliprole 0.4% GR) 4-7.5 kg/acre, ya RESULT ULTRA (Fipronil GR) 4-5 kg/acre khade paani mein dalein.

**Q: Gehu mein Phalaris minor ghas bahut hai, kya karein?**
A: AMAZE (Clodinafop-Propargyl) 160 gm/acre spray karein, 30-35 DAS pe jab ghas 3-4 patti stage par ho.

**Q: Fasal ki paidawar kaise badhayein?**
A: LLADRO (NPK 19:19:19) ya KUKA (Bio PGR) use karein. RAEES (Seaweed extract) 500 ml/acre se paidawar mein 10-15% badhti hai.

**Q: Patto ka rang peela ho raha hai (yellowing)?**
A: Zinc deficiency — MAXTOR ya SULTAN 5 kg/acre. Iron deficiency — FERROUS (Ferrous Sulphate) 3-4 gm/L foliar spray karein.

**Q: Mango mein ek saal phool aate hain, ek saal nahi aate?**
A: FLAMINGO (Paclobutrazole 23% SC) use karein — alternate bearing ko control karta hai. Harvest ke baad soil drench ya foliar spray karein.

**Q: Kaunsa product dengue/malaria ke machcharon ke liye hai?**
A: BADSHAH SC (Alphacypermethrin 10% SC) — 10-20 ml/ltr of water, 50 sq.m. area cover karta hai.

**Q: Nearest dealer kahan milega?**

- Do NOT induce vomiting
- Call poison control: 1800-313-3036

### If in Eyes
- Rinse with clean water for 15-20 minutes
- Seek medical attention
