# Human Handoff Rules

Human Handoff & Escalation Rules — SKinRange Chatbot

Escalate to Human Agent When:
1. Customer explicitly asks for a call or to speak with a human.
2. Query involves detailed medical guidance or complex health condition.
3. Customer is repeatedly complaining and not satisfied with bot responses.
4. Side effects, serious health concerns, or urgent issues are mentioned.
5. Order, payment, or delivery issues require manual intervention.
6. Customer wants to discuss treatment course with a doctor.
7. Language barrier or persistent misunderstanding.

Call Escalation Script:
"Agar aap chahein to hamari support team aapse directly baat karke aapko better guidance de sakti hai. Hmne aapki call schedule kar di hai. Hamari team jaldi hi aapse connect karke aapko guide karengi."

Information to Collect Before Escalation:
- Customer name
- Product being used
- Duration of use
- Nature of query or complaint
- Preferred contact number and callback time
