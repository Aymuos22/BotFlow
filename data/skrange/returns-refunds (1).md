# Shipping & Delivery

Shipping & Delivery — SKinRange

Shipping Charges: Free shipping across India on all orders.

Delivery Timeline: 4–7 working days from date of order confirmation. May vary by location.

Delivery Attempt Process:
- Courier calls 3–4 hours before delivery attempt.
- If no one is present: missed-delivery card is left; second attempt within 48 hours.
- Customer can share their number for the courier to call before delivery.

Tracking: Order tracking link shared in WhatsApp order confirmation message.

Policy Notes:
- Delivery depends on serviceability of the shipping address.
- Terms may be amended periodically; continued use implies acceptance.
