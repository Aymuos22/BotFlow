# Company Overview

SKinRange is an established Ayurvedic healthcare company focused on delivering authentic, natural, and result-oriented wellness solutions. The company operates with deep respect for classical Ayurvedic science while addressing modern lifestyle-related health concerns through practical and personalised approaches.

SKinRange does not position itself as a generic herbal seller. Instead, it functions as a long-term healthcare partner, supporting individuals throughout their wellness journey with guidance, education, and natural solutions.

Vision: To become a globally trusted name in Ayurvedic healthcare by combining ancient wisdom with modern understanding and responsible innovation.

Mission: To improve quality of life by offering safe, effective, and personalised Ayurvedic solutions that help people manage health conditions naturally and sustainably.

Core Values: Authenticity (genuine Ayurvedic formulations), Integrity (honest communication, no exaggerated claims), Holistic Healing (root-cause treatment), Quality & Safety (GMP & ISO certified manufacturing), Customer Well-Being (long-term support over short-term sales).

Company History: Started as a focused Ayurvedic initiative, evolved into a trusted healthcare brand with national presence across India via digital platforms. Now has international offices in Dubai and the United States, serving millions of customers.

Target Audience: People facing addiction (alcohol, smoking, drugs); Type 2 diabetes patients; joint pain / arthritis sufferers; men and women seeking sexual wellness and vitality; individuals with hair, skin, gut, kidney, liver, weight, and respiratory concerns.
