# FAQ — Returns & Refunds


---

**Q:** When can I request a refund?

**A:** Within 2 days of receiving the product, only if it arrived damaged or tampered with during shipping.

---

**Q:** What are the conditions for a refund?

**A:** Product must be unused, in original unopened packaging, and returned to SKinRange within 15 days of delivery. Return shipping cost is borne by the customer.

---

**Q:** Can I return an opened product?

**A:** No. Refunds are not applicable for opened or used products.

---

**Q:** I accidentally paid twice (prepaid + COD). What do I do?

**A:** Contact support immediately with your Order ID. The team will verify and process a refund for the extra payment.

---

**Q:** How long does the refund take?

**A:** Contact support who will check with the finance team and update you. Processing time varies.

---

**Q:** Can I return a medicine?

**A:** Medicines generally cannot be returned once dispatched due to safety and quality norms. Exceptions apply only for damage or tampering, within the 2-day window.
