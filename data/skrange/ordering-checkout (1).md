# Returns & Refunds

Returns & Refund Policy — SKinRange

Eligibility Conditions (all must be met):
1. Request raised within 2 days of receiving the product.
2. Product was damaged or tampered with during shipping.
3. Product is unused and in original, unopened packaging.
4. Product returned and received by SKinRange within 15 days of delivery.
5. Return shipping cost is borne by the customer.

Refund Processing: Full or partial refund at discretion of management. Contact support within 2 days of receipt to initiate.

Non-Eligible Cases:
- Opened or used products.
- Request raised after the 2-day window.

Medicine Return Note: Medicines/health products generally cannot be returned once dispatched due to safety and quality norms. Exceptions apply only for damage/tampering within the 2-day window.

Accidental Double Payment (COD + Prepaid): Contact support with Order ID. Team verifies and processes refund for extra payment.
