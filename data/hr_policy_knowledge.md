# Royal Crop Science (RCS) - HR Policies & Employee Handbook

Welcome to the official HR Knowledge Base for Royal Crop Science (RCS), also known as General Crop Science (GCS). This document contains the policies, procedures, and benefits for our employees.

## 1. Company Identity & Culture
Royal Crop Science (RCS) is an industry leader in agrochemicals. We value integrity, innovation, and "Farmer's Smile." Our workspace is professional yet supportive, fostering a culture of continuous learning.

## 2. Working Hours & Attendance
- **Standard Hours**: 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM (Monday to Saturday).
- **Lunch Break**: 1:00 PM to 2:00 PM.
- **Attendance**: Employees must mark their attendance using the biometric system or the official HR mobile app.
- **Late Arrivals**: Three late arrivals (more than 15 minutes) in a month will result in a half-day salary deduction.

## 3. Leave Policy
- **Casual Leave (CL)**: 12 days per year. (Maximum 2 days at a time).
- **Sick Leave (SL)**: 8 days per year. Medical certificate required for more than 2 consecutive days.
- **Privilege/Earned Leave (PL)**: 15 days per year, credited after 1 year of service. Can be carried forward up to 45 days.
- **Maternity Leave**: 26 weeks as per statutory laws.
- **Paternity Leave**: 5 days.
- **Public Holidays**: RCS follows a list of 12 declared public holidays per year.

## 4. Salary & Benefits
- **Payroll Cycle**: Salaries are processed by the 7th of every month.
- **Provident Fund (PF)**: Applicable as per government norms.
- **ESIC**: Health insurance coverage for eligible employees.
- **Annual Bonus**: Performance-linked bonus distributed during the Diwali season.
- **Incentives**: Sales and manufacturing teams are eligible for performance-based incentives.

## 5. Travel & Reimbursements
- **Conveyance**: Employees traveling for official work are eligible for KM-based reimbursement (2-wheeler/4-wheeler).
- **Outstation Travel**: Requires prior approval from the department head. TA/DA rates depend on the city tier (A, B, C).
- **Claims**: All reimbursement claims must be submitted with original bills via the HR portal by the 25th of the month.

## 6. Code of Conduct
- **Dress Code**: Formals/Semi-formals (Mon-Fri), Smart Casuals (Sat).
- **Confidentiality**: Employees must not disclose proprietary formulation or client data.
- **Anti-Harassment**: RCS has a zero-tolerance policy for any form of harassment. Complaints can be reported directly to the ICC (Internal Complaints Committee).

## 7. Performance Management (PMS)
- **Annual Appraisal**: Conducted in April every year.
- **Probation**: New joiners are on probation for 6 months. Confirmation is subject to performance review.

## 8. Resignation & Notice Period
- **Notice Period**: 1 month for employees, 3 months for senior management/technical heads.
- **Exit Interview**: Mandatory for all resigning employees.
